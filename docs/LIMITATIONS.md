# Residual gates

- First-party Python plugin/host bridge contributions are MIT by explicit owner
  confirmation; third-party notices and firmware licensing remain separate.
- Independent source/security/catalog review of exact final hashes remains.
- Serial SDK dependency closure is not installed or hardware-qualified here.
- No physical USB/RF/firmware/audio/model-quality or consumer installation proof.
- Dedicated Pocket-only home only; unconditional execution veto is incompatible
  with general-purpose/shared/multiplexed profiles.
- Schema-empty selection is observed installed-version behavior, not a future
  public no-schema guarantee. The registered execution veto is the safety layer.
- Same-UID malicious processes/admins, deliberate hook removal/core replacement,
  and hostile unreviewed plugins are outside this trusted-profile boundary.
- Unknown outcomes are not retried automatically. Bridge delivery acceptance is
  not proof of RF reception or audible playback.
- No public repository/push/PR/catalog action or production change was made.
